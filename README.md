### About me

I build practical software and data products, from an early prototype through integration and evaluation. My experience connects applied AI, business intelligence and full-stack development, with a focus on turning a real need into a working, understandable solution.

- Developing Python and computer-vision prototypes, including a facial-recognition access-control system evaluated with an ROC curve.
- Building data warehouses, ETL workflows and BI reports with SQL, SSIS, Power BI and SSRS.
- Creating web applications and connecting APIs, databases and application components.

I am completing my engineering degree in Data Science, Big Data and Artificial Intelligence at ENSIASD. I am looking for a six-month end-of-study internship starting in January 2027.
